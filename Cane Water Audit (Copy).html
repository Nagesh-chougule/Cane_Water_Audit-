Cane Water Audit

Example data. Overwrite with your plant's readings.

Plant and flow data

Enter a season-average day from your meters and logbooks. All flows are in m³ per day unless stated. Switch the unit with the m³/day and L/day buttons at the top; the figures you type are converted for you. Everything else in the audit recalculates as you type, and your entries are kept in this browser.

Outlet quality against limits

Enter the latest lab results at the sugar ETP outlet and the distillery CPU or condensate polishing outlet. Limits are indicative placeholders. Replace them with the values in your Consent to Operate and the standard for your disposal or reuse route.

Drought stress test

Cut the fresh water available to the plant and see how long storage lasts and how much of the gap the adopted action plan closes.

Audit report

A detailed PDF report with the process diagram, action plan and solar PV study, built live from the data you have entered, or a plain-text summary to copy.
